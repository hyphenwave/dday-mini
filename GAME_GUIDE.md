# 🌍 World PvP Game Guide

Welcome to World PvP, the ultimate competitive token game on Solana! This guide will help you understand the game mechanics and develop winning strategies.

## 🎯 Game Objective

**Goal**: Help your chosen country achieve the highest market cap within 30 days to unlock a nuclear missile and eliminate another country from the game.

**Ultimate Victory**: Be the last country standing after 211 rounds of competition!

## 🏗️ Core Mechanics

### 1. Country Selection
- Choose from 211 countries worldwide
- Each country has its own unique token
- Start with equal market cap of $0
- Build your country's economy through token purchases

### 2. Token Economics
- **Bonding Curve**: Initial pricing mechanism for fair distribution
- **Market Cap**: Determined by total token supply and demand
- **Uniswap Deployment**: Automatic when reaching $333k market cap
- **Trading**: Buy/sell tokens on the platform or Uniswap

### 3. President System
- **Election**: Top token holder becomes president automatically
- **Powers**: Control nuclear missile targeting if country wins
- **Crown**: Visual indicator (👑) in chat and leaderboard
- **Overthrow**: Can be dethroned by larger token holders

## 💣 Nuclear Missile System

### How to Get a Nuke
1. **Win a Round**: Achieve highest market cap in 30 days
2. **Automatic Unlock**: Nuke becomes available immediately
3. **One-Time Use**: Must be used before next round starts

### Nuke Effects
- **Target Elimination**: Permanently removes country from game
- **Liquidity Rugging**: Drains target's liquidity pool
- **Reward Distribution**: 
  - 50% to winning country (buybacks)
  - 50% to random country on map
- **Population Reduction**: Active countries decrease by 1

### Strategic Considerations
- **Target Selection**: Choose wisely - can't be undone
- **Alliance Building**: Coordinate with other countries
- **Defensive Strategy**: Build strong token holdings to avoid targeting
